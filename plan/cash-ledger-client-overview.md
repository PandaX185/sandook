# Cash Ledger App — Features Overview

**For:** sandook project
**Prepared:** August 2026

---

## What is this?

A simple, secure program that replaces the three Excel files currently used for:

1. **Car parking** — daily bills and monthly bookings
2. **Petty cash** — the custody money (top-ups and takings)
3. **Shop cash & deposits** — daily sales and bank deposits

Everything is in **one place**, works on **phone, tablet, and computer**, and the
numbers are calculated automatically — no more manual math, no more mistakes.

---

## Who can use it?

Each person gets their own **username and password**.

- **Editors** — can enter and change data, and manage the list of users
- **Viewers** — can look at everything and open reports, but cannot change anything

Every entry is recorded with **who did it and when** — so you always know where
any number came from.

---

## 1. Car Parking

- **Enter each bill** in seconds: car plate number + amount + cash or card
- **Totals automatically** for: today, yesterday, this week, this month, or any
  custom date range — cash and card shown separately
- **Search any plate number** to see its full history
- **Daily cash statement**: opening cash, money transferred to the shop, staff
  salaries, expenses, closing cash — like the current sheet, but calculated for you
- **Monthly bookings**: list of subscribed plates with their monthly rate and
  renewal month — the app reminds you which renewals are due

## 2. Petty Cash

- **Top up** (add money to the custody) or **take** (record an expense)
- The **running balance is always correct** — calculated automatically from the
  entries, never typed by hand
- Each entry has a description so the history is clear

## 3. Shop Cash & Deposits

- **Daily sheet**: sales, extra income, withdrawals, deposits — same columns as
  the current file
- **Cash-in-hand balance** computed automatically for every day
- **Deposit check**: if a deposit doesn't match the cash, the app flags it
  immediately — no more silent differences discovered months later
- **Petty cash link**: when money is taken from the drawer for petty cash, it is
  recorded in **both** the shop sheet and the petty cash ledger automatically

## 4. Parking ↔ Shop Transfers

- When money moves from parking to the shop (or back), it is recorded **once**
  and appears correctly in **both** ledgers — no double entry

## 5. Reports

- Daily, monthly, and all-time totals for every section
- **Export to Excel** whenever you want the numbers in a file

---

## What changes for the better?

| Before (Excel) | After (App) |
|---|---|
| Manual sums — small errors creep in | All totals calculated automatically |
| Files copied between devices — versions get mixed | One system, always up to date, cloud-saved |
| Anyone can edit anything, no trace | Private logins, permissions, full history |
| Mistakes found weeks later | Mismatches flagged the same day |
| Works only on one computer | Works on phone, tablet, and computer |

## One page summary

- 🚗 **Parking**: bills, daily totals, cash statements, monthly bookings
- 💵 **Petty cash**: custody ledger with automatic balance
- 🧾 **Shop cash**: daily sheet with automatic balance and deposit checks
- 🔄 **Transfers**: parking ↔ shop, recorded once, reflected everywhere
- 📊 **Reports**: today / yesterday / week / month / custom — with Excel export
- 👥 **Users**: editor & viewer roles, secure login, full history

*All amounts in AED, with the system ready to support other currencies in the future.*
